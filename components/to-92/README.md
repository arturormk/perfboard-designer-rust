# TO-92, 3 pins

A TO-92 package with its three leads 2.54 mm apart, for small transistors, low-power regulators such as the 78L05 and sensors. Leads formed at 1.27 mm are bent out to fit.

The pins are named Pin 1 to Pin 3, from the left with the flat face towards you, because the pinout depends on the part (E-B-C, C-B-E, IN-GND-OUT, …). To name them, copy the part to the project and rename its pins.
