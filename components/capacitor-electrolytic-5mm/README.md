# Electrolytic capacitor, 5 mm

A radial electrolytic capacitor 5 mm across, standing on the board. Its leads (2.0 mm, spread apart on the part) go into holes 2.54 mm apart. Holes under the can, other than the leads, are occupied.

It is polarized: the positive lead is pin 1 at the origin, and the negative lead, on the side of the stripe, is pin 2.

The definition has no value. Record it in the placed part's notes.
