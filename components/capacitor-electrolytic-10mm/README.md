# Electrolytic capacitor, 10 mm

A radial electrolytic capacitor 10 mm across, standing on the board. Its leads (5.0 mm apart on the part) go into holes 5.08 mm apart. Holes under the can, other than the leads, are occupied.

It is polarized: the positive lead is pin 1 at the origin, and the negative lead, on the side of the stripe, is pin 2.

The definition has no value. Record it in the placed part's notes.
