# Electrolytic capacitor, 8 mm

A radial electrolytic capacitor 8 mm across, standing on the board. Its leads (3.5 mm, squeezed apart on the part) go into holes 2.54 mm apart. Holes under the can, other than the leads, are occupied.

It is polarized: the positive lead is pin 1 at the origin, and the negative lead, on the side of the stripe, is pin 2.

Give each placed part its capacitance as its value, such as 100n, 4.7u, 22p or the code 104.
